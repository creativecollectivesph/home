Website privacy.

This page explains how the Creative Collective inquiry form handles the information you choose to submit.

Project inquiries

The form collects the details you enter, which can include your name, email address, organization, requested service, estimated budget, timeline, and project description. Creative Collective uses this information to review your inquiry and respond.

Form submissions are relayed to CreativeCollectivesPH@gmail.com by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. FormSubmit states that submitted form data may be retained for 30 days. Review FormSubmit’s privacy information before submitting if you need more detail.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, health information, or other highly sensitive information through this form.

Cookies and analytics

This website’s code does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and artwork are served locally. FormSubmit is contacted only when you submit the inquiry form.

Hosting

When this site is hosted, the hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details. Its practices depend on the provider selected by the site owner.

Contact

Questions about submitted information can be sent to CreativeCollectivesPH@gmail.com.
